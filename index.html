Through a structured curriculum designed for children from preschool to Grade 8, as well as beginner-level learners, we help students develop their Tamil language skills, deepen their understanding of their heritage, and build a lasting connection to their cultural roots.

Why Choose Arivakam Tamil Cultural Academy?

At Arivakam, we believe learning Tamil is about more than language. It is about understanding our history, celebrating our culture, and staying connected to our roots. Through structured learning and a shared love for Tamil heritage, we help the next generation carry their language and traditions forward.

Structured Syllabus

As a registered non-profit educational organization, Arivakam is dedicated to providing quality Tamil education to the next generation. Our structured curriculum supports students from preschool to Grade 8, helping them develop their Tamil reading, writing, and language skills while deepening their understanding of Tamil culture, history, and heritage.

International Examinations

Through the International Curriculum for Educational Development of Tamils (ICEDT), Arivakam provides a standardized Tamil education curriculum followed across 14+ countries. Students participate in internationally coordinated final examinations and comprehensive assessments in speaking, listening, reading and writing, allowing them to demonstrate their knowledge, strengthen their language skills and track their academic progress.

Students, families, and the wider community come together to celebrate occasions such as Pongal, Tamil Heritage Month ceremonies, and other cultural events throughout the year. These celebrations bring our community closer, promote cultural awareness, and help preserve Tamil traditions and heritage for future generations.

Prize-Giving & Graduation Ceremonies

We celebrate our students’ dedication, achievements, and progress through annual prize-giving ceremonies and graduation celebrations, recognizing milestones such as Grade 3, Grade 6, and Grade 8 graduations. Parent & Student Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about student enrollment, curriculum grade levels, weekend schedules, and academic certificates.

When Do Your Classes Take Place? expand_more
Class times depend on which school you join. Our classes can take place in the evenings during weekdays, or mornings, afternoons or evenings on weekends.
My Child Did Not Learn Tamil When They Were Younger, Can They Still Join? expand_more
Yes! We accept students from preschool to grade 8. Students will be evaluated by their teacher on the first day to see their grade level. For students who are beginning their Tamil education at an older age, we have beginner friendly books.
How Can I Check The Progress Of My Child? expand_more
We hold Parent Teacher Conferences to ensure parents are up to date with their child’s progress in our schools. If you have questions about your child’s progress you can contact your students' teacher or our head office anytime.
Do You Have Online Classes? expand_more
Yes, we offer in-person and online classes.
I Feel My Child Needs More Support In Class. expand_more
If you notice your child is experiencing difficulty, please contact us so we can see how to best support your child.
Can I Change My Child’s School? expand_more
Yes. If you would like to change your child's school please contact our head office.
Do You Have Training For Your Teachers? expand_more
Yes! We have seminars and training for our teachers with topics ranging from: Tamil, teaching and technology.
How Do I Become A Teacher At Arivakam? expand_more
Please click here to register to become a teacher and our head office will contact you.
When Do Your Workshops, Exams, Events And Competitions Take Place? expand_more
You can find important dates in your child’s agenda, as well as “Upcoming Events” section on our website.
How Do I Register My Child For Tamil School? expand_more
Click here to complete the student registration form. Once the form is complete, our head office will contact you.
Do You Offer Adult Tamil Classes? expand_more
No. Arivakam’s focus is teaching students preschool to grade 8.
