Parent & Student Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about student enrollment, curriculum grade levels, weekend schedules, and academic certificates.

how_to_reg Enrollment & Admissions

How do I register my child for classes? expand_more
You can easily register online through our Student Registration page during our Fall (August) and Winter (December) open enrollment terms, or visit any of our 39 regional hub locations across North America.
What is the minimum starting age for enrollment? expand_more
Children must be at least 4 years old by December 31st of the academic year to enroll in our Preschool (Mazhalai) level.
Are classes available for beginners with no prior Tamil exposure? expand_more
Yes! We offer structured introductory streams tailored for children with zero prior exposure to Tamil. Our certified instructors use supportive bilingual (English-Tamil) techniques to build vocabulary, pronunciation, and confidence step-by-step.

school Academic Schedule & Curriculum

How often do classes take place? expand_more
Classes take place once a week, typically on Saturday or Sunday mornings, for 2-3 hours depending on the student's grade level.
Can students attend online or in-person? expand_more
We offer both learning pathways! In-person weekend classes operate across our 39 regional hub centers in Ontario, Quebec, Western Canada, and the United States, while structured virtual online classes are available for remote families.
Are homework assignments and evaluations required? expand_more
Students receive lightweight weekly practice assignments to reinforce reading and writing skills. Mid-term assessments and end-of-year evaluations take place twice annually to ensure academic progression.

verified Certificates, Fees & Volunteering

Are official academic certificates provided upon completion? expand_more
Yes! As a registered non-profit educational institution, Arivakam issues formal grade completion certificates at the end of each academic year and an official Diploma upon Grade 8 graduation.
How can high school students volunteer or teach with Arivakam? expand_more
High school students can join our Volunteer Program as classroom assistants, earn certified community service hours, and progress into teaching assistant roles upon graduation.
