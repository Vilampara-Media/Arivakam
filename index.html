FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I register my child for classes? expand_more
You can easily register online through our Student Registration Form during open enrollment, or visit any of our 39 regional campus hubs.
What is the starting age for Preschool (Mazhalai) level? expand_more
Children must be at least 4 years old by December 31st of the academic school year to enter our Preschool (Mazhalai) stream.
Are classes available for beginners with no prior Tamil exposure? expand_more
Yes! We offer supportive introductory streams designed specifically for diaspora children starting with zero prior language exposure.
