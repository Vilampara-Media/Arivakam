Section 1

Our Non-Profit Commitment & Educational Scope

Arivakam Tamil Cultural Academy operates as a registered non-profit educational institution dedicated to Tamil language, literature, arts, and cultural heritage across North America since 2008. We understand that parents and educators place deep trust in our academy when enrolling children in our weekend schools, speech festivals, and cultural events.

This Privacy Policy sets forth our standardized data governance practices pursuant to the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial education privacy guidelines. It applies to all interactions across our website, registration portals, school locations, and official academy competitions.

Section 2

Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data required to effectively administer Tamil curriculum instruction, schedule student classes, and maintain school safety. Such information includes:

face Student Information

Student full name, date of birth, age, current academic grade in regular day school, and prior Tamil reading/writing level.

contact_phone Parent & Guardian Details

Parent/guardian legal names, primary telephone numbers, email addresses, residential city, and designated emergency contacts.

school Teacher & Volunteer Profiles

Educator qualifications, background check verifications (Vulnerable Sector Checks), teaching experience, and preferred campus assignments.

receipt_long Enrollment & Fees Records

Tuition receipts, course level enrollment records, and competition participation logs. We do not retain credit card numbers on local web servers.

Section 3

How Personal Information Is Used

Personal information collected is exclusively used for educational purposes:

  • check_circle Classroom Placement: Assigning children into suitable Tamil instructional levels (Preschool, Grades 1 to 8, or Diploma).
  • check_circle Direct Communication: Sending class schedules, syllabus updates, teacher feedback, and emergency weather closures to guardians.
  • check_circle Cultural Events & Contests: Organizing student speech, spelling, and essay competitions, including issuing certificates of excellence.
  • check_circle Volunteer Certification: Providing high school students with authenticated community service hour verification letters.

Section 4

Strict Non-Disclosure & Zero-Sale Guarantee

block We Never Sell or Rent Personal Information

Arivakam Tamil Cultural Academy has operated since 2008 on an uncompromised principle of community service. We have never sold, rented, leased, or traded parent or student phone numbers, email addresses, or demographic data to commercial marketing firms, lead generators, or third-party advertisers.

Information is only disclosed when legally compelled by court orders or where emergency medical safety requires contacting primary healthcare responders during school hours.

Section 5

Student Media, Photography & Video Policy

During annual cultural celebrations, Pongal festivals, Tamil speech contests, and Grade 8 graduation ceremonies, academy photographers capture commemorative photos and video footage.

  • verified Guardian Consent: Media release permissions are explicitly requested during enrollment in our student registration form.
  • verified Opt-Out Privilege: Parents may request an opt-out at any time during the school year without affecting their child's academic standing or participation.
  • verified Takedown Procedure: If you wish to remove any published photograph of your student from our website or community album, notify our Head Office at info@arivakam.org and it will be promptly removed within 48 business hours.

Section 7

Parental Rights & Access Requests

Parents and legal guardians maintain full rights under Canadian privacy legislation to review, update, or request deletion of their child's records on file with Arivakam Tamil Cultural Academy.

To submit an access or correction request, please email our administrative registrar at info@arivakam.org with your child's student name and school campus. Requests are verified and fulfilled at no charge.
