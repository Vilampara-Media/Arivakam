📜 Moral Values & Heritage

Kids' Thirukkural Wisdom Compass

Written over 2,000 years ago by Saint Thiruvalluvar, Thirukkural teaches universal ethics. Explore friendly bite-sized couplets explaining friendship, education, kindness, and truth!

📜 Chapter: Friendship (அதிகாரம்: நட்பு)
Kural #782
செயற்கரிய யாவுள நட்பின் அதுபோல்
வினைக்கரிய யாவுள காப்பு.
Simple Kid Translation:

"What is so difficult to acquire as true friendship? What is such a noble shield against harm?"

💡
Kid's Daily Moral Action:

Be a loyal, encouraging companion to your classmate or sibling today. True friends lift each other up!

🛡️ Parent Peace of Mind

Academic Excellence You Can Trust:
Preschool to Grade 8 Milestones

While children experience learning through play, parents benefit from an internationally vetted syllabus recognized in 14 countries. Here is how your child progresses year by year:

1

Preschool & Kindergarten (Ages 4–6)

Foundation Stage

Focus on phonics, picture flashcards, alphabet recognition, conversational Tamil at home, and cultural rhymes.

2

Primary Grades 1 to 5 (Ages 6–11)

Milestone Exam 1

Sentence construction, grammar rules, spelling bee mastery, storytelling, reading fluency, and Grade 3 oral/written evaluation.

3

Middle School Grades 6 to 8 (Ages 11–14)

Diploma Graduation

Classical Thirukkural analysis, formal speeches, Tamil creative essays, and prestigious Grade 8 Graduation Diploma award.

Parent Assurance Guarantee

"What if we only speak English at home?"

Do not worry! Over 70% of our incoming students begin with limited conversational exposure. Our educators are bilingual in English and Tamil, creating a warm, zero-pressure atmosphere where kids gain confidence within the first 4 weeks.
