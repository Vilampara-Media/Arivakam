Vibrant & Fun Learning Programs

At Arivakam, learning goes far beyond textbooks. Kids experience Tamil heritage through interactive classes, speech contests, art bees, stage showcases, and summer games!

🎓 Academic Zone

1 Interactive Classroom

  • ✓ Preschool to Grade 8: Structured classes for kids aged 4 to 14.
  • ✓ International Syllabus: Globally recognized curriculum in 14 countries.
  • ✓ Oral & Written Exams: Continuous reading & speaking assessment.
  • ✓ Grade 3, 6 & 8 Graduation: Diploma milestones for students.
🗣️ Oratory Stage

2 Speech & Confidence Stage

  • ✓ Speech Competitions (பேச்சுப் போட்டி): North American Oratory Contests.
  • ✓ Spelling Bee (சொல்லாட்சி): Vocabulary building & spelling challenges.
  • ✓ Annual Prize Givings: Trophies, medals & certificates of excellence.
  • ✓ Public Speaking: Building lifelong leadership and courage.
🎨 Creative Art

3 Art & History Exhibitions

  • ✓ Art Contests (ஓவியப் போட்டி): Creative drawing celebrating nature & heritage.
  • ✓ Student Exhibitions: Displays of student models, charts & artwork.
  • ✓ Literature Projects: Ancient Thirukkural & Tamil story projects.
  • ✓ Creative Expression: Encouraging artistic imagination in Tamil.
🎪 Festivals & Games

4 Festivals, Drama & Sports

  • ✓ Vaakai Stage Programs: Annual drama, dance & music performances.
  • ✓ Tamil Heritage Month: Special January celebrations & Pongal festivities.
  • ✓ Summer Sports Meets: Traditional Tamil outdoor games & family picnics.
  • ✓ In-Person & Online: Campus centers & live Zoom virtual classrooms.

help Frequently Asked Questions

When do classes take place?
Classes take place during weekday evenings or weekend mornings/afternoons depending on campus.
Child has no prior Tamil knowledge?
No problem! Students are evaluated on day one and placed in beginner-friendly levels tailored to their age.
What syllabus is followed?
We use the International Diaspora Tamil Syllabus taught in 14 countries worldwide.
Are online classes available?
Yes, we offer both in-person local school centers and virtual online Zoom classrooms.
